# Landscape vocabulary

- **Landscape**: the procedural world determined by a seed and its painting settings.
- **View**: the rectangular part of a landscape selected by camera position and zoom.
- **Painting settings**: the mountain, water, vegetation, atmosphere, and color choices that determine the landscape's appearance.
- **Render session**: one canvas's current landscape, view, and readiness to draw, from asynchronous creation through disposal.
- **Capture**: a PNG together with the settings and dimensions of the frame it records.
- **Device loss**: the end of a render session's ability to draw, requiring a new session rather than restoration of the old one.
